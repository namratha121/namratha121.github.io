Academic project · Customer Analytics

Beyond the Swipe

Interactive Tableau dashboards that turn raw dating app data into a clear story of who users are, how they behave, and what drives likes, matches, and engagement.

Course
Customer Analytics, UC San Diego
Tools
Tableau, data preparation, BI dashboards
Team
Prabhlin Kaur Matta, Abhigna Akkipeddi, Chandana Vuppu, Namratha K, Pavithra Vaidyanathan
View full deck ↗ Open dashboard ↗

Goals

  • Understand user personas to improve matching, personalization, and overall user experience.
  • Give product, marketing, and UX teams a clear view of who their users are.
  • Help users optimize their profiles and engagement.

Data

  • CSV files covering user profiles, behaviors, and engagement metrics.
  • Joined profiles with behavior data on user ID into one analysis-ready dataset.

Key findings

WHO THE USERS ARE
  • Most users are 18 to 30, with a balanced sex split.
  • Relationship intentions range from casual to serious.
  • Younger users swipe more selectively; older groups swipe more optimistically.
WHAT MAKES A PROFILE WORK
  • 3 to 5 photos get the most likes.
  • Any bio triples likes, and short bios perform best.
  • Verified users get 2.2x more likes.
WHEN USERS ENGAGE
  • Activity peaks at 12 PM; swiping is heaviest late at night.
  • 10 to 15 minute sessions produce the highest match rates.
  • Female engagement is steadier; male engagement peaks more sharply.
WHERE MATCHES DROP OFF
  • The biggest leak is early, at the swipe and first-chat stage.
  • Users who stay active and keep messaging churn less and earn more mutual matches.

Impact

Product and UX teams can use the dashboard to spot where matches are dying and which behaviors define healthy users, then design experiments that push more people into that healthy pattern.
