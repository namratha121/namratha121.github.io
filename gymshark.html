Gymshark AI Coworker

An end-to-end, AI-assisted influencer marketing platform for Gymshark. It discovers and scores YouTube fitness creators, evaluates audience and brand-safety risk, drafts personalized outreach, generates product briefs, and analyzes campaign performance, all through a Next.js dashboard backed by FastAPI.

How it works

STEP 01

Creator discovery & scoring

  • Reads creator profiles from creators.csv.
  • Scores on brand fit, authenticity, niche alignment, engagement, audience size, and tier-specific thresholds.
  • Applies disqualifiers like competitor loyalty, brand-safety concerns, clickbait, or existing partnerships.
  • Produces a ranked shortlist.
STEP 02

Sentiment & risk analysis

  • Processes comment data from comment_sentiment.csv.
  • Uses VADER-scored sentiment to calculate positive, negative, neutral, purchase-intent, and flagged-comment rates.
  • Classifies each creator as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or UNKNOWN risk.
STEP 03

Outreach drafting

  • Combines creator data, CRM records, tier, niche, deal type, and brand guidance.
  • Uses RAG retrieval from a Gymshark knowledge base for brand context.
  • Validates drafts for tier-appropriate tone, word limits, and banned phrases.
STEP 04

Human review gates

  • Reviewers approve shortlisted creators, outreach drafts, and responses before the workflow moves on.
  • Keeps AI recommendations under human approval instead of fully automating partnership decisions.
STEP 05

Product brief generation

  • Runs after a creator accepts outreach.
  • Recommends a product line based on the creator's niche and audience.
  • Generates a campaign brief, creative direction, deliverables, and promo code.
STEP 06

Campaign analytics

  • Reads results from campaign_performance.csv.
  • Calculates ROI, conversion rate, cost per click, revenue, engagement, and top and bottom performers.
  • Breaks results down by creator tier and deal type.

APIs & services

FastAPI
Backend REST API for creators, analytics, outreach drafts, product briefs, risk data, and campaign actions.
Next.js
Frontend dashboard for Discovery, Outreach, Analytics, and Product Briefs.
LLM API
Optional TritonAI/OpenAI-compatible API for AI-generated outreach and briefs, with template-based fallbacks when no key is available.
RAG retrieval
Pulls relevant Gymshark partnership and brand documents to ground generated outreach.
MCP servers
Tools for creator scoring, risk checks, outreach drafting, and structured creator and campaign resources.
VADER
Local NLP library for comment sentiment classification (not an external API).

Data used

creators.csv
Curated creator prospect profiles and scoring attributes.
comment_sentiment.csv
VADER-scored YouTube comment records.
crm_records.csv
Simulated CRM pipeline and relationship data.
hypeauditor_intake_log.csv
Audience-quality and fraud-risk intake fields.
existing_partners.csv
Existing Gymshark partner records.
campaign_performance.csv
Synthetic campaign performance results.
outreach_responses.csv
Simulated yes/no creator responses used to test the workflow.

The creator and comment datasets are structured research inputs stored locally. Campaign outcomes and outreach responses are simulated for testing.
