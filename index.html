About

I'm drawn to the why behind buying: why a customer converts, why they churn, and what a brand can do about it.

I'm finishing my MS in Business Analytics at UC San Diego and bring 2 years of experience across analytics roles at Ralph Lauren, Mazda North American Operations, MetroPlusHealth, and Erthe Golf.

My work spans customer segmentation, campaign and funnel analysis, predictive modeling, and AI-powered automation. I'm drawn to businesses that work with customers head-on. I'm looking for data and business analyst roles in marketing, media, e-commerce, and retail, in any industry that uses consumer behavior and data to drive growth.

I’m naturally curious and I like understanding how things work, which is probably what drew me to data in the first place. I enjoy work that gives me something to figure out, especially when there isn’t an obvious answer.

Outside of work, I love hiking, thrifting, discovering new coffee shops (and complaining that the coffee isn’t strong enough), and getting overly competitive about board games (I’m a sore loser). My ideal Sunday would be a morning hike, a bagel and coffee, then a good book on the beach.
